Dr.-Ing. Alireza Malekmohammadi

Postdoctoral Researcher · Brain Networks, Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences, Leipzig

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences in Leipzig. My research investigates the mechanisms underlying the effects of transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) on the human motor cortex, using advanced electrophysiological measurement techniques combined with biophysical modeling.

I received my Dr.-Ing. (Ph.D.) in Electrical Engineering from the Technical University of Munich (TUM), where I worked at the Institute for Cognitive Systems on a Brain Sound Computer Interface: using machine and deep learning to decode sound sequences from the human brain.

Before that, I was a research assistant at the University of Tübingen (2017–2018), studying the effects of intra-operative deep brain stimulation of the subthalamic nucleus on cortical physiology in Parkinson’s disease. I received my M.Sc. in Digital Electronics Engineering from Sharif University of Technology (2015), where I designed efficient FPGA implementations of machine learning methods for a motor-imagery BCI, and my B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from Shahid Beheshti University (2013).
