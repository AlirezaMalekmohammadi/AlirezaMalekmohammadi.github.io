Dr.-Ing. Alireza Malekmohammadi

Postdoc Researcher · Brain Networks, Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences

Alireza Malekmohammadi is a postdoctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences in Leipzig.

Focus: Investigating the mechanisms underlying transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) effects on the human motor cortex using advanced electrophysiological measurement techniques combined with biophysical modeling.

He finished his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), Department of Computer Engineering. He worked on the "Brain Sound Computer Interface" (utilizing deep and machine learning methods to decode sound sequences from the human brain) at the Institute for Cognitive Systems (ICS, https://www.ei.tum.de/ics/people/malekmohammadi-alireza/).

He received his bachelor’s degree in “Electrical Engineering” from Shahid Beheshti University, Tehran, Iran 🇮🇷, in 2013, under the supervision of Prof. Mohammad Eshghi. In 2015, he earned his M.Sc. in “Digital Electronics Engineering” from Sharif University of Technology in Iran 🇮🇷. During his master's, he joined the Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) group under Prof. Mahdi Shabany, developing an efficient design and hardware implementation of machine learning methods (i.e., SVM, LDA, KNN, ANN) for a motor-imagery-based BCI system on a Virtex-6 FPGA. From 2017 to 2018, he spent one year as a research assistant at Tübingen University in Germany 🇩🇪. He studied the effects of intra-operative monopolar deep brain stimulation of the subthalamic nucleus on cortical physiology in Parkinson’s disease.
